      About this map

      This map shows places in India where people can design and build things: makerspaces, tinkering labs, FabLabs and prototyping centres. Every entry was checked against a source, and each popup says how well it is verified and how precise its pin is.

      What is on the map

        How each entry is checked

        Verified
        Confirmed on the organisation's own website or an official government or university record.
        Partly verified
        Confirmed by a semi-official source (for example the operator's network list or Wikipedia), or the official source gives only part of the address.
        Unverified
        Listed by a directory or third-party site, but not yet confirmed by the place itself.

        How pins are placed

        Addresses were turned into coordinates with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim. A pin is placed on the building or campus only when the name matches and it lies in the right state and within 25 km of its PIN code. Otherwise the pin falls back to the PIN-code area or town centre and is drawn faded. Some pins were checked by hand.
